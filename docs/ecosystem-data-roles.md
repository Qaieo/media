# Qaieo ecosystem responsibilities

## Ecosystem data responsibilities — October 25, 2026

Omid's current direction: **Toekyn generates verified-work data. Qaieo owns simulation, reinforcement learning (RL), evaluations and machine-learning (ML) datasets/models, combining verified-work data with simulation data. Yeki combines these inputs and research outputs with external API data for underwriting and purchase-order/milestone financing workflows.**

The four planned government sources are **SAM.gov Opportunities, SAM.gov Contract Awards, USAspending and SAM.gov Entity Management**. These are four feeds across SAM.gov and USAspending, not four additional APIs beyond SAM.gov. The October 25 integration plan is design only: collectors and signals are not activated. Do not describe the complete production data path as live.

Keep physical observations, reviewed/verified work, simulation outputs, model estimates and independent outcomes separately identifiable, with source lineage, availability cutoffs and purpose-specific rights. Observed, reviewed, verified, customer-accepted and paid remain distinct. A shared source or a simulation derived from a TVW/API record is not independent corroboration. Public award data does not prove execution, customer acceptance or payment.

Current demonstrations use synthetic data; financing remains in shadow mode. This role clarification activates no collector, real-data ingestion, external training, credit-policy change or financial action.
