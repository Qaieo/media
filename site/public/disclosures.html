Disclosures

Who stands behind Qaieo

Qaieo Frontier is a publication from Yeki (yeki.ai), a company building financing for signed contracts at hardware companies. Qaieo covers awards, milestones and contracts shaping the physical economy, including grants and prizes. Companies covered by Qaieo may also fall within Yeki's financing focus; the conflict and public-source rules below apply to that coverage.

How conflicts are handled

  • Any company with which Yeki has a term sheet, a loan, or an active diligence process is flagged at the top of every Qaieo post that covers it.
  • Information Yeki receives from companies in diligence or as borrowers never feeds Qaieo coverage. Qaieo writes from public sources only.
  • This page is updated whenever a new relationship starts or ends.

Current relationships

None at this time.

Not advice

Nothing on Qaieo is investment, legal, or financial advice.
